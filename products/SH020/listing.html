Our Heritage of Craftsmanship

Welcome to our artisanal workshop where traditional shoemaking meets modern design. Located in Chengdu, China, our factory combines generations of craftsmanship expertise with contemporary fashion sensibility to create footwear that stands apart from mass-produced alternatives.

Every pair of shoes we produce is a testament to the skill of our master craftsmen. Using time-honored techniques like Goodyear welt construction and hand-stitching, we create footwear that not only looks beautiful but is built to last. We believe in quality over quantity, craftsmanship over shortcuts.

Our commitment to excellence means we source only the finest materials - premium full-grain leathers, soft sheepskin linings, and durable soles. Each shoe undergoes rigorous quality control to ensure it meets our exacting standards before reaching your customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is your minimum order quantity?

A: Our standard MOQ is 30 pairs per color. For larger orders, we offer better pricing tiers at 100+ and 500+ pairs.

Q: Can I get samples before bulk order?

A: Yes, we offer sample production. Sample fee is typically 2x the unit price plus shipping. Sample time is 7-10 working days.

Q: What customization options are available?

A: We offer custom logo (embossed/engraved/metal), custom colors, custom materials, custom heel heights, custom packaging, and more.

Q: What is your quality control process?

A: Every pair undergoes multi-point inspection including material check, construction review, and final quality assessment before shipping.

Q: Do you offer dropshipping services?

A: Yes, we can fulfill orders directly to your customers with blind packaging and custom branding.

Q: What payment terms do you accept?

A: For new clients, we typically require 30% deposit and 70% balance before shipping. T/T, PayPal, and Alibaba Trade Assurance are accepted.
